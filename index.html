The philosophy

Small steps can lead to meaningful change.

You do not need a perfect plan or a dramatic transformation. Choose one useful idea, try it in everyday life, and learn from the result. Then decide on your next step.

The Better Books philosophy is simple: read with curiosity, act on what matters, and keep adjusting as you learn.

About the author

Meet Smart Richard.

Smart Richard is an independent author and maker of practical learning tools. The Better Books project brings together ideas about clearer thinking, stronger memory, lifelong learning and living well.

The guiding belief is that useful knowledge should be put to work in everyday life—and that progress can begin with one small step.
